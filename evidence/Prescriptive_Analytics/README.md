# Prescriptive_Analytics — prescriptive analytics (synthetic proof of concept)
Run `python Prescriptive_Analytics.py /path/to/Supply_Chain_CSV /path/to/Uncertainty_Quantification/tables` from this directory. For fresh orders, first generate Uncertainty_Quantification forecasts then run `python score_new.py orders.csv predictions.csv tables/validation_residual_source.csv output.csv`. Python dependencies: numpy, pandas, matplotlib. Replay verified identical actions and costs for all 1,000 original scoring orders. Random draws depend on batch order/size; identical batch order is required for exact replay.

## Method
600 common Monte Carlo draws select the minimum expected cost among six intervention families, with four candidate buffer quantiles (50,80,90,95%) when applicable. 1,000 separate draws evaluate fixed base policy recommendations in each stress scenario. Primary lead times are predicted plus bootstrap residuals from validation by sea/non-sea, or actual historical test lead times for retrospective evaluation. Daily demand is a single lognormal scenario rate with observed mean and CV, not independent day-by-day draws. Alternative quoted lead time gets lognormal noise (log SD .2); expediting saves uniform .5–1.5 times quoted savings. Minimum one day. Moderate stress adds 7 sea/3 non-sea days, 3 alternative days and 15% demand; severe doubles these shocks. Stress scenarios are assumptions, not probabilities. No reoptimisation under stress. Raw classifier probabilities are displayed only; simulated late probabilities come from lead-time draws.

## Policies and costs
Maintain; ERP buffer (planned demand plus existing target safety stock less available inventory); uncertainty buffer (90% upper interval times daily demand less available inventory); optimised (maintain, buffer, expedite, alternate, expedite+buffer, alternate+buffer). All emergency buffers capped at 14 days mean demand and assumed immediately available. Full alternate sourcing requires quoted capacity >= order quantity and distinct supplier. No splitting. Per-order expected relevant cost = pre-receipt holding integral + unmet units times shortage penalty + alternative price difference times quantity + expediting fee + 20% of primary unit cost times emergency buffer units. Common primary purchase cost excluded; buffer acquisition principal excluded assuming future use; working capital and post-receipt costs not modelled. This is not total landed cost or realised savings. Negative alternative cost differences are allowed. Shortage is potential pre-receipt unmet demand, not verified stockout. Orders treated independently, so no aggregation as enterprise savings, shared stock/capacity or budget constraints, replenishment interactions, cancellation fees or recovery-time claims. Actual supplier acceptance not verified. Simulation and evaluation share distributional assumptions; previous validation/test reuse limits external validity. No SAP or Shiny deployment; no real data. Sensitivity to assumptions and independent prospective evaluation needed before use.
